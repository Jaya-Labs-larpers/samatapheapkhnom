Three directions for /contact, all built from SKO's own design system

Same content in all three: welcome title and body, email, phone and hours, address with map link, Donate link, and the same form. INHF gave the ideas (depth from a photo band, a clear "how to reach us" moment, warmer surfaces). The look comes from SKO: white surfaces with hairlines, rationed brand blue, aqua/sky tints, the butterfly from the logo, and real SKO photos. Desktop only for now. Mobile comes once you pick one.
